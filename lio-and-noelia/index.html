About the App

Lio & Noelia is a charming children's story about an elephant and a butterfly who become friends throughout the course of the tale.

This children's story features beautifully animated illustrations that bring each page of the story to life. Children can touch interactive elements in the illustrations, each triggering an immersive animation.

For an even more immersive experience, the app offers a full narration of the story. By touching a flower in the text box, children can enjoy the story being lovingly read aloud.

An interactive experience that will delight children and parents alike.
