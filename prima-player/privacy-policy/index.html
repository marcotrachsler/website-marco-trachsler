Privacy Policy for Prima Player

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is processed when you use Prima Player, a music player for training, teaching, and rehearsals. It covers the app and support communications about the app. It does not cover the separate operation of the website on which this notice is published.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

Marco Trachsler is responsible for the processing described in this policy (the data controller).

For privacy questions or requests, contact marcotrachsler@outlook.com.

In this policy, “we” refers to Marco Trachsler and “you” refers to the person using Prima Player.

2. Music and data stored on your device

Prima Player accesses your music library or selected audio files to provide playback and library features. Depending on your device and the features you use, the app may request permission to access your music library or audio storage. You can change these permissions in your device settings. Refusing or withdrawing access may prevent the corresponding features from working.

The app stores data locally, including music metadata such as titles, artists, albums, file references and artwork references; favorites, playlists and markers; playback settings and presets; and information such as when a track was last played. It also stores subscription status information and the time of the last successful online subscription confirmation to support temporary offline access.

Prima Player does not upload your audio files, music library, playlists, markers or listening history to us or to RevenueCat. Your device's operating system, backup service or the provider of your music may separately process data under its own privacy policy. Local app data may be included in device backups depending on your settings.

3. Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Prima Player uses RevenueCat, Inc. to manage subscriptions and free trials, verify access, and restore purchases. The app does not require you to create a separate Prima Player account. RevenueCat assigns a randomly generated app user identifier. This identifier is pseudonymous: it is not your name or email address, but it can be associated with your purchases.

Subscription processing involves purchase receipts or tokens, transaction and product identifiers, purchase history, subscription and trial status, renewal and expiration information, and technical information such as platform, operating system, app version and service interaction times. Network requests also expose your IP address to the receiving service.

We use this information to provide paid access, resolve purchase problems and understand subscription performance through RevenueCat's reporting tools. We do not send your music or local playback history to RevenueCat.

Payments are handled by Apple's App Store or, on Android, Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card details. The stores process payment and account information under their own policies.

Further information:

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

RevenueCat processes app customer data on our behalf under its data processing terms. Its general privacy policy also describes activities relating to its own website and developer customers that do not necessarily apply to Prima Player users.

4. Error reports and support

Prima Player records errors locally to help diagnose problems. Reports can include the error time, error message, technical stack trace, app version, operating system and device model. Error messages may also contain file names, paths or other information involved in the error. The app keeps up to 50 diagnostic entries and removes older entries as new ones are recorded. You can delete entries in the app's diagnostic screen.

These reports are not automatically sent to us. If you choose to copy a report or send it by email, you decide what to share. The email function opens your email app so you can review the message before sending it.

If you contact us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use this information to respond and troubleshoot. Our email provider also processes the correspondence to deliver and store it.

5. Advertising and tracking

Prima Player does not display advertisements or include advertising tracking SDKs. We do not sell personal data or share it for targeted advertising. The app does not include a separate behavioral analytics or automatic crash-upload service. RevenueCat still processes subscription and technical data as described above.

6. Why we process personal data

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: providing the app's requested functionality, validating subscriptions, restoring purchases and handling related support.
  • Legitimate interests: maintaining a reliable service, diagnosing technical problems, understanding subscription performance and protecting against misuse. These interests are balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligations: retaining or disclosing information where applicable law requires it.
  • Consent: where required for optional processing. You may withdraw consent for future processing without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Store payment processing is governed by the relevant store's own legal bases. Subscription verification data is necessary to provide paid access; without it, we may be unable to activate or restore your subscription. Support emails and sharing diagnostic reports are optional.

7. Recipients and international processing

Data is shared with service providers only as needed for the purposes described here, including RevenueCat and its hosting providers, the applicable app store, and our email provider when you contact us. We may also disclose information where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

RevenueCat is based in the United States and uses hosting infrastructure there. Subscription data can therefore be processed outside your country, including outside Switzerland, the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. Store and email providers may also process information internationally under their own terms.

RevenueCat's Data Processing Addendum provides contractual safeguards for relevant international transfers, including standard contractual clauses and provisions for Switzerland and the United Kingdom. You can contact us for information about the safeguards applicable to your data.

8. How long data is kept

Local library data and settings remain on your device until removed or until the app's local data is deleted. Uninstalling the app generally removes its local data, but operating-system backups, secure storage and files outside the app may remain under the relevant platform's controls. Diagnostic entries are subject to the limit described above.

Subscription records may remain after cancellation or uninstalling because they are needed to verify and restore purchases, resolve transaction issues and meet applicable legal requirements. They are not automatically deleted when you remove the app. Support correspondence is retained for as long as necessary to address the request, related follow-up and applicable legal claims or obligations. You may request deletion of data held by us; legal retention duties or other applicable exceptions may limit deletion.

Deleting data or uninstalling Prima Player does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your app store.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or a portable copy. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Contact marcotrachsler@outlook.com to make a request. We may need information to verify your identity and locate the relevant subscription record, because Prima Player does not require a named account. Do not send payment card details or account passwords.

You may complain to the competent data protection authority, including the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, your local EU/EEA authority or the UK Information Commissioner's Office, as applicable.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data processing changes. The latest version will be available on this page with an updated date. Where required, we will provide additional notice of material changes.
